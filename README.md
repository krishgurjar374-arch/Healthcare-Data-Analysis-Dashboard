# Healthcare-Data-Analysis-Dashboard

Healthcare data analysis and dashboard project using Microsoft Excel





\## 📊 Project Overview



A comprehensive \*\*Healthcare Data Analysis Dashboard\*\* developed using \*\*Microsoft Excel\*\* to analyze healthcare data across clinical, financial, patient, and operational dimensions.



The project transforms raw healthcare records into meaningful KPIs, PivotTable-based analysis, visualizations, and dashboard views to support data-driven healthcare insights.



\---



\## 🎯 Project Objectives



The main objectives of this project are to:



\* Analyze healthcare and patient data

\* Identify important healthcare KPIs

\* Understand clinical and operational patterns

\* Analyze healthcare costs and payments

\* Examine patient demographics and hospital performance

\* Create structured analytical reports

\* Present insights through an interactive Excel dashboard

\## 📈 Dashboard \& Analysis



The project is organized into four major analytical areas.



\### 1. 🏠 Overview Analysis



Provides a high-level summary of the healthcare dataset, including:



\* Total patient/visit activity

\* Hospital distribution

\* Patient demographics

\* Blood group distribution

\* Geographic distribution

\* Overall healthcare trends



\### 2. 🩺 Clinical Analysis



Analyzes clinical characteristics and patient outcomes, including:



\* Primary diagnoses

\* Secondary diagnoses

\* Disease severity

\* Treatment outcomes

\* Mortality indicators

\* Hospital-level clinical patterns



\### 3. 💰 Financial Analysis



Provides insights into healthcare financial performance, including:



\* Total healthcare charges

\* Medicine costs

\* Laboratory costs

\* Surgery/procedure costs

\* Insurance payments

\* Patient payments

\* Payment distribution

\* Financial KPIs



\### 4. ⚙️ Operational Analysis



Analyzes healthcare operations and patient experience, including:



\* Admission types

\* Referral sources

\* Patient satisfaction

\* Length of stay

\* ICU utilization

\* Operational performance

\## 🔍 Key Business Questions



The project helps investigate questions such as:



1\. Which hospitals have the highest patient volumes?

2\. What are the most common diagnoses?

3\. How are patients distributed across hospitals and locations?

4\. What patterns exist in disease severity and treatment outcomes?

5\. Which hospitals show higher mortality levels?

6\. What are the major components of healthcare costs?

7\. How much of healthcare expenditure is covered by insurance?

8\. How much is paid directly by patients?

9\. Which referral sources contribute the most patients?

10\. How does patient satisfaction vary?

11\. What patterns exist in length of stay and ICU utilization?

12\. What operational and clinical areas require further investigation?
